# NCT02864966 — A 24 Hour Occlusive Single Patch Test to Assess Acute Tolerance of Pre Lubricated Condom on a Panel of Healthy Adult Subjects

> This is a 24 hour patch test to determine the acute tolerance of 3 variants of condom on a panel of healthy adult subjects. The patches will be affixed to the back and remain under occlusion for 24 hours. They will then be assessed at 30 minutes and 24 hours post patch removal. (NCT NCT02864966; status COMPLETED).
