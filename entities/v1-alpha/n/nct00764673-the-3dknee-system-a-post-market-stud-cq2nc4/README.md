# NCT00764673 — The 3DKnee™ System: A Post-Market Study

> The purpose of this study is to evaluate the survivorship and efficacy of the Encore 3DKnee™ system in a group of patients who will receive the 3DKnee™ as a primary (first) orthopedic implant in the knee. Each knee will be designated as a separate subject and followed for a period of 2 years. (NCT NCT00764673; status COMPLETED).
