# NCT05002205 — Long-term Effects of COVID-19: a Comparative Cohort Study

> This study is a longitudinal cohort study which investigates the effect of COVID-19 in ambulatory care. This study aims to assess the effect of COVID-19 beyond the acute phase, i.e. on long-term symptoms, respiratory and cardiovascular health, use of health services, and quality of life.
