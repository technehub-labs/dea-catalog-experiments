# NCT01863108 — Safety Study of a Dendritic Cell-based Cancer Vaccine in Melanoma

> The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the safety and tolerability of multiple sub-cutaneous injections of GeniusVac-Mel4, a dendritic cell-based cancer vaccine, in patients with melanoma. The secondary objectives are to determine immune response and clinical efficacy of such injections in patients with melanoma. (NCT NCT01863108; status COMPLETED).
