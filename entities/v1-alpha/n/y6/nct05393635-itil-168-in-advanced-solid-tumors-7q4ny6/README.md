# NCT05393635 — ITIL-168 in Advanced Solid Tumors

> DELTA-2 is a phase 1 clinical trial to evaluate the safety, feasibility, and preliminary efficacy of ITIL-168 with pembrolizumab in participants with advanced cancer whose disease has progressed after standard therapy. ITIL-168 is a cell therapy derived from a patient's own tumor-infiltrating immune cells (lymphocytes; TILs). (NCT NCT05393635; status WITHDRAWN).
