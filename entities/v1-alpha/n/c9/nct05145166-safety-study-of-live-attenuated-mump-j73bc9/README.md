# NCT05145166 — Safety Study of Live Attenuated Mumps Vaccines in Junior High School Students Under 14 Years Old

> This study of live attenuated mumps vaccines is an observational study in which active monitoring and passive monitoring were combined to conduct safety observation.The live attenuated mumps vaccines manufactured by Sinovac (Dalian) Vaccine Technology Co., Ltd.The purpose of this study is to evaluate the safety of live attenuated mumps vaccines after large-scale application and accumulate safety data for the application of live attenuated mumps vaccines,and provide scientific basis for the formulation of vaccine immunization prevention strategies.
