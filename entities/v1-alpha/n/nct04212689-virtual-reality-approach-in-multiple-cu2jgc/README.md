# NCT04212689 — Virtual Reality Approach in Multiple Sclerosis

> The aim of our study is to investigate the effects of game-based virtual reality exercise added to conventional physiotherapy and rehabilitation program in patients with Multiple Sclerosis (MS). In order to evaluate its effectiveness, assessment of pain, range of motion and disability will be applied. (NCT NCT04212689; status UNKNOWN).
