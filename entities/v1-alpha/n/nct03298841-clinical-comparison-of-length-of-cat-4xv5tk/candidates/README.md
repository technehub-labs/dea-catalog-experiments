# NCT03298841 — Clinical Comparison of Length of Cataract Procedures With Zeiss Lumera Versus Older Zeiss Microscope

> Study design will be a mix of retrospective (older Zeiss microscope previously in use at our surgery center) and prospective data collection with the Lumera and Callisto software. The Lumera and Callisto software system will be used for all cataract surgeries including toric analyses and lens placement. (NCT NCT03298841; status COMPLETED).
