# NCT05787860 — Ruxolitinib in Seborrheic Dermatitis

> This study is an open-label prospective interventional trial that will assess the efficacy of ruxolitinib in the treatment of seborrheic dermatitis. It will also attempt to characterize the molecular immune profiles of patients with SD at week 0 and week 4, with comparison to baseline profiles in healthy control subjects. (NCT NCT05787860; status COMPLETED).
