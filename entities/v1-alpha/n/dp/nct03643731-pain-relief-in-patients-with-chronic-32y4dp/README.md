# NCT03643731 — Pain Relief in Patients With Chronic Low Back Pain Using HeatTens vs. Standard Care.

> A two-armed randomized controlled clinical trial with blind assessments and a follow-up period of 4 weeks is developed. The aim of this study is to investigate the effects of the combination of heat and TENS (HeatTENS device) on pain in people with chronic low back pain. A sample of 70 patients will be recruited. Following baseline measurements, subjects will be randomly allocated to the experimental or the control group. The experimental group will be asked to use the device on a daily basis, 30 minutes per day. The control group will have no device. After 4 weeks of FU, measurements will be repeated. (NCT NCT03643731; status COMPLETED).
