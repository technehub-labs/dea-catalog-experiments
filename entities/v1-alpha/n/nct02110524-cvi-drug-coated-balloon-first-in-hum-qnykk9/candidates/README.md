# NCT02110524 — CVI Drug Coated Balloon First In Human Trial

> The purpose of this study is to assess safety and the inhibition of restenosis of the CVI Paclitaxel-coated PTA Balloon Catheter in the treatment of de-novo occluded/stenotic or reoccluded/restenotic superficial femoral (SFA) or popliteal arteries. The primary endpoint for this analysis is late lumen loss at six (6) months. (NCT NCT02110524; status COMPLETED).
