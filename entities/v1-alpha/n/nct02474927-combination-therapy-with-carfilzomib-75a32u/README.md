# NCT02474927 — Combination Therapy With Carfilzomib for the Antibody-Mediated Rejection Diagnosis in Lung Transplantation

> The clinical trial is a Phase II open label, single-arm pilot study to evaluate the safety and efficacy of combination therapy with carfilzomib, plasma exchange and intravenous immunoglobulins for AMR after lung transplantation and elucidate important clinical and immunologic phenotypes and mechanisms associated with these outcomes.
