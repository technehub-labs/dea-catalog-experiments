# NCT00213369 — Neuromodulation of Aspiration Secondary to Stroke

> Aspiration could induce major respiratory affection. They are often present after stroke. The aim of this study is to facilitate swallowing recuperation in stroke patients using peripheral laryngeal stimulation and to understand its mechanisms using motor evoked potential and cortical evoked potential. (NCT NCT00213369; status COMPLETED).
