# NCT01086969 — A Study of Meningococcal Vaccine, Menactra® in Healthy Subjects in India

> The purpose of this study is to assess the safety and immunogenicity of a single dose of Menactra® vaccine to support registration. Primary Objectives: * To describe the antibody titers measured by serum bactericidal activity using baby rabbit complement (SBA-BR) before and after Menactra® vaccination. * To describe the safety profile of participants after one dose of Menactra®. (NCT NCT01086969; status COMPLETED).
