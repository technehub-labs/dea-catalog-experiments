# NCT05745935 — Role of Local Skin Incision Infiltration by Oxytocin On Wound Healing

> caesarean section rate in Egypt has reached about 60% between primgravidae. Wound complications are of the most common morbidities following cesarean section. The prevalence of wound infection and disruption after cesarean has been reported as 3-15%, averagely speaking 6% and 2-42% in various studies. These complications affect mother's quality of life due to stress, anxiety, delay in mother's ability and health recovery (NCT NCT05745935; status UNKNOWN).
