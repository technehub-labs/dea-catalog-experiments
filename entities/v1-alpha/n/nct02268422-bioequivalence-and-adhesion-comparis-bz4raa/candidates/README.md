# NCT02268422 — Bioequivalence and Adhesion Comparison of Buprenorphine Patches

> To compare a 2nd generation Buprenorphine Transdermal System (BTDS) patch with a marketed 1st generation BTDS patch to confirm that the two are bioequivalent (deliver the same amount of drug) and that they equally both stick to the skin over 7 days of continuous wear. (NCT NCT02268422; status COMPLETED).
