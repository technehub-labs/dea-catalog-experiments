# NCT04228653 — Long-Term Follow-up Safety After DDS Implantation With/Without CDNF Infusions

> This study is a follow up to the HP-CD-CL-2002 clinical study. It evaluates the long-term safety in patients with Parkinson's disease after implantation of an investigational drug delivery system (DDS) with or without infusions of CDNF. All patients will have at least the port explanted. (NCT NCT04228653; status UNKNOWN).
