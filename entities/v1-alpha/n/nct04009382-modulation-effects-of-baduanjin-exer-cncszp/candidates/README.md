# NCT04009382 — Modulation Effects of Baduanjin Exercise on Subjective Cognitive Decline

> The aim of this study is to investigate the modulation effects of Baduanjin (BDJ), a simple and innovative mind-body exercise, on cognitive function, resting state functional connectivity, and brain morphometry in individuals with subjective cognitive decline (SCD). The results obtained will provide novel insights for improving the prevention of age-related cognitive decline and Alzheimer's disease. (NCT NCT04009382; status COMPLETED).
