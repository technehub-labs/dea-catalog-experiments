# NCT07449429 — AI-Assisted Coronary Syndrome Subtyping From Admission History of Present Illness: A Clinician Reader Study

> This study will evaluate whether an OCR-LLM-based clinical decision support framework improves clinicians' accuracy in classifying coronary syndrome subtypes from admission history of present illness. Participating clinicians will classify a set of cases first without AI assistance and then with assistance from the OCR-LLM framework. The primary outcome is the change in classification accuracy between unaided and AI-assisted assessments. (NCT NCT07449429; status COMPLETED).
