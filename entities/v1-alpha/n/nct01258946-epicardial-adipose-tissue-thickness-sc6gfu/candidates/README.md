# NCT01258946 — Epicardial Adipose Tissue Thickness in Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)

> The aim of the present study was to search whether epicardial adipose tissue thickness, a predictor of increased cardiovascular risk, increases in PCOS patients or not and, if it does, to analyze the relationship of EATT with insulin resistance, adiponectin and biochemical and anthropometric parameters.
