# NCT07141550 — Kinesthetic Brain and Telerehab Pilates for Cognitive and Functional Outcomes in Women With Fibromyalgia

> Fibromyalgia is a chronic pain syndrome often accompanied by fatigue, psychological problems, and cognitive dysfunction, leading to reduced quality of life. Telerehabilitation-based Pilates has been shown to improve symptoms and function in fibromyalgia, while kinesthetic brain exercises may enhance cognitive performance through neuroplasticity. This study aims to evaluate the effects of adding kinesthetic brain exercises to telerehabilitation-based Pilates on cognitive functions, symptom management, functional performance, and quality of life in women with fibromyalgia. (NCT NCT07141550; status NOT_YET_RECRUITING).
