# NCT02332161 — Leg Function and ADL After ALT Reconstruction for Head and Neck Cancer

> Anterolateral thigh (ALT) free flap tissue transfer is a commonly used method of head and neck reconstruction after head and neck cancer removal. The procedure involves removing some muscle, skin, and tissue from the thigh, and this may affect leg function. The purpose of this study is to determine the impact of ALT on ambulation and activities of daily living (ADLs) (NCT NCT02332161; status COMPLETED).
