# NCT05419479 — Switch Maintenance in Pancreatic

> This study is being done to test the safety and effectiveness of combining domvanalimab (AB154), zimberelimab (AB122), and APX005M with pancreatic cancer that has spread to other parts of body. This research study involves immunotherapy. Immunotherapy triggers the body's immune system to fight cancer cells.
