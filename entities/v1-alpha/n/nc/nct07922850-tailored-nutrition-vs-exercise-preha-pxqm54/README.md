# NCT07922850 — Tailored Nutrition vs. Exercise Prehabilitation Before Colorectal Cancer Surgery

> This study will test whether tailored nutritional support before surgery, alone or combined with a structured exercise program, can help adult patients recover better after surgery for colorectal (bowel) cancer. Participants will be randomly assigned to one of three groups: usual care, tailored nutritional support alone, or nutritional support combined with a structured exercise program (multimodal prehabilitation). The main goal is to find out whether either approach improves physical function 30 days after surgery, measured by how far participants can walk in 6 minutes (the 6-minute walk test). The study will also look at nutritional intake, physical function, quality of life, complications, hospital readmissions, and length of hospital stay. A total of 140 adult patients scheduled for surgery for colorectal cancer at hospitals in Northern Norway will be enrolled. (NCT NCT07922850; status NOT_YET_RECRUITING).
