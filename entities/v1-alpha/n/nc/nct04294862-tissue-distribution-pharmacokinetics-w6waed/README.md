# NCT04294862 — Tissue Distribution, Pharmacokinetics, Safety, and Tolerability After a Single Dose of TNP-2092 in Participants Undergoing Primary Total Hip or Knee Arthroplasty

> This study will investigate TNP-2092 distribution into joint tissues in participants undergoing a total hip arthroplasty (THA) or a total knee arthroplasty (TKA). Tissue distribution is the primary objective, and plasma pharmacokinetics (PK), safety, and tolerability are secondary objectives. (NCT NCT04294862; status COMPLETED).
