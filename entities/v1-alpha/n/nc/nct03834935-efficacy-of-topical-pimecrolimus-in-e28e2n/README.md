# NCT03834935 — Efficacy of Topical Pimecrolimus in the Treatment of Pityriasis Alba

> Pityriasis alba (PA) is a common benign skin disorder, that usually affects children and adolescents of darker phototypes. A history of atopic dermatitis is a well-known risk factor, and PA may be a minor manifestation of atopic dermatitis, although it can occur in nonatopic individuals as well. The objective is to evaluate the effect of topical pimecrolimus in the treatment of treatment of PA (NCT NCT03834935; status UNKNOWN).
