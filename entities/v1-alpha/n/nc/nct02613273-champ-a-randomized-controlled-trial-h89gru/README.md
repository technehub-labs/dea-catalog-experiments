# NCT02613273 — CHAMP: A Randomized Controlled Trial of High-intensity Aerobic and Resistance Exercise for Metastatic Prostate Cancer

> This Phase II, open-label, three-arm randomized controlled trial (RCT) will investigate the effects of 12 weeks of aerobic exercise vs. 12 weeks of resistance exercise vs. usual care in men with metastatic castration resistant prostate cancer to determine the safety, feasibility, and tolerance of exercise; quality of life indicators, and a prognostic score. (NCT NCT02613273; status COMPLETED).
