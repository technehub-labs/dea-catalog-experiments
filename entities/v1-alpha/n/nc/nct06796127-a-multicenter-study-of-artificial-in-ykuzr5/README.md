# NCT06796127 — A Multicenter Study of Artificial Intelligence Model for Fetal Congenital Heart Disease

> This study addresses congenital heart disease in fetuses, a specific birth defect, by collaborating with multicenter medical institutions across China. It prospectively collects echocardiographic screening, diagnostic images, and structured parameter data from both normal and abnormal fetuses. Cases are followed up, and artificial intelligence (AI) models are developed to extract fetal heart echocardiographic image features and analyze the data. This process allows for the analysis of the AI models' accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity, and verifies the application value of AI models in fetal heart echocardiographic screening and diagnosis. (NCT NCT06796127; status NOT_YET_RECRUITING).
