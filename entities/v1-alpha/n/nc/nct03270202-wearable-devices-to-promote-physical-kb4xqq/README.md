# NCT03270202 — Wearable Devices to Promote Physical Activity

> This study evaluates the effect of a new wearable device, that measures physical activity level based on heart rates, on relevant health outcomes including cardiorespiratory fitness. Half of participants will be instructed in using the Mio Slice wristband, while the other half will be requested to follow today's recommendations for physical activity. (NCT NCT03270202; status TERMINATED).
