# NCT01001169 — Safety and Immunogenocity Study of GSK Biologicals' Pandemic Influenza (H1N1) Candidate Vaccine in Japanese Children

> The objective of this study is to evaluate the immunogenicity and safety of GSK Biologicals' investigational influenza vaccine GSK2340274A following one dose and following a second dose, using the same dosage as has been used in the H5N1 development program in Japanese children aged 10-17 years and an alternative dose in children aged 6 months to 9 years. (NCT NCT01001169; status COMPLETED).
