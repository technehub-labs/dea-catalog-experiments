# NCT05627973 — Health Status of < 50 Year Old Patients After Surgically Implanted Biological and Mechanical Aortic Valves: a Population-based Retrospective Analysis (2010-2020)

> The overall project aim is to study outcomes following aortic valve replacement with either mechanical valves or bioprostheses by retrieving data from the main social security carriers in Austria for the years 2010-2020 in under 50 year old patients. (NCT NCT05627973; status COMPLETED).
