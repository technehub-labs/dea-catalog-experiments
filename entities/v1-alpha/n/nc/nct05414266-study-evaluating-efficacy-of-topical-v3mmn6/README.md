# NCT05414266 — Study Evaluating Efficacy of Topical Squaric Acid Dibutyl Ester in Children and Adolescents With Alopecia Areata

> The purpose of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and tolerability of squaric acid dibutylester (SADBE) in children and adolescents with alopecia areata (AA) and to evaluate effect of squaric acid dibutyl ester on patient-centered outcomes and payer relevant measures to assess treatment benefit from the patient perspective and to demonstrate value (NCT NCT05414266; status UNKNOWN).
