# NCT05468073 — Therapeutic Evaluation of Low-dose IL-2-based Immunomodulatory Approach in Patients With Early AD

> Study aims at evaluating the therapeutic efficacy and safety of low-dose IL-2 immunomodulatory treatment in patients with early AD, in a phase II, randomized, double blind, placebo-controlled phase II clinical trial. Patients with AD at early stage will be recruited and randomized (2:1) in each treatment group. The primary endpoint is the rate of decline assessed through CDR change at 18 months between the placebo group and the treated patients. (NCT NCT05468073; status ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING).
