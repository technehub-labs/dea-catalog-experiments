# NCT02019940 — A Pilot Study Of Riluzole In Patients With Post Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

> This open label study will evaluate the safety and efficacy of riluzole in patients with PTSD. Patients will receive riluzole 50mg twice per day orally for 12 weeks as outpatient, with a one month follow up at week 16. (NCT NCT02019940; status COMPLETED).
