# NCT01828424 — Prediction of Postoperative Pain by Measuring Nociception at the End of Surgery

> There is a large variability of postoperative pain intensity and of the drug doses necessary to alleviate this pain. The investigators hypothesis is that a measurement of nociception at the end of surgery, using either the RIII reflex threshold, measures of heart rate variability or the pupil dilatation reflex measured by pupillometry, in relation to the doses of opioids used intraoperatively will yield a prediction of postoperative pain. (NCT NCT01828424; status COMPLETED).
