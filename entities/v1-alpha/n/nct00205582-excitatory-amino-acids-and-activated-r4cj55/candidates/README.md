# NCT00205582 — Excitatory Amino Acids and Activated Microglia After Traumatic Brain Injury: a (R)-[11C]PK11195 PET Study

> Excitatory amino acids may be involved in secondary neuronal damage after traumatic brain injury. The amount of microglia activation is an indirect measure of neuronal damage. Micorglia activation will be measured R)-\[11C\]PK11195 PET 1 week, 1 month and 6 months after brain injury. (NCT NCT00205582; status UNKNOWN).
