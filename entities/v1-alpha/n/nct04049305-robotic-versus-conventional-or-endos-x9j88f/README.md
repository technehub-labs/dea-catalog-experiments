# NCT04049305 — Robotic Versus Conventional or Endoscopic Nipple Sparing Mastectomy for Breast Cancer

> This study will retrospectively collect and evaluate the surgical outcomes of robotic nipple sparing mastectomy (R-NSM) compared with endoscopic assisted NSM (E-NSM) or conventional NSM (C-NSM) in the management of breast cancer. Multi-centers pooled data analysis would be performed for comparisons of R-NSM compared with C-NSM or E-NSM. (NCT NCT04049305; status UNKNOWN).
