# NCT02556970 — A Study Investigating Pain Following Single-port or Multi-port Video Assisted Thoracoscopic Surgery

> This study seeks to establish if thoracoscopic surgery performed through a single port or incision reduces early post-operative pain compared with conventional multiple port thoracoscopic surgery. This initial pilot study is designed to establish whether a trial of the two techniques is acceptable to patients, clinically feasible and can be delivered in a reasonable timescale. (NCT NCT02556970; status WITHDRAWN).
