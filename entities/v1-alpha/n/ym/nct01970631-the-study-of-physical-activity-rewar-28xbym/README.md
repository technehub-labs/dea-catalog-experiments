# NCT01970631 — The Study of Physical Activity Rewards After Knee Surgery

> The objectives of this research are to conduct a proof of concept randomized controlled trial with 200 patients undergoing primary total knee replacement (TKR) at Brigham and Women's Hospital (BWH). The trial will compare levels of physical activity in subjects in the behavioral and economic interventions versus "Usual Care" post TKR. (NCT NCT01970631; status COMPLETED).
