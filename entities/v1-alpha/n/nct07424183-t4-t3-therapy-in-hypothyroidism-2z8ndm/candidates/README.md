# NCT07424183 — T4/T3 Therapy in Hypothyroidism

> This study will test whether 6 months of combination therapy with levothyroxine (LT4) plus liothyronine (LT3) is superior to LT4 plus placebo in participants with hypothyroidism who have residual symptoms of hypothyroidism with TSH levels within the reference range.
