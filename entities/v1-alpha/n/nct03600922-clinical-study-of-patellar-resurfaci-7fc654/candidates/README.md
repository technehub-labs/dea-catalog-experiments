# NCT03600922 — Clinical Study of Patellar Resurfacing and Non-resurfacing in Total Knee Arthroplasty

> The literature regarding resurfacing of the patella in total knee arthroplasty has conflicting conclusions. This study assesses medium-term clinical results of total knee arthroplasties performed with and without the patellar resurfacing in patients undergoing single-staged bilateral total knee arthroplasty in a randomized controlled, clinical study at a minimum of 5 years follow-up. (NCT NCT03600922; status COMPLETED).
