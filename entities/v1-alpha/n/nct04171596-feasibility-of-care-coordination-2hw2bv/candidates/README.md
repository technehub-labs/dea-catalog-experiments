# NCT04171596 — Feasibility of Care Coordination

> The primary aim of this study is to determine feasibility of incorporating a primary care coordinator (PCC) into an integrative concierge primary care practice. Secondary aims include: 1. To determine whether the PCC intervention increases patient satisfaction and/or patient activation. 2. To determine whether regular telephone contacts from a PCC over a period of 6 months increases the number of visits and services by DIPC members. (NCT NCT04171596; status COMPLETED).
