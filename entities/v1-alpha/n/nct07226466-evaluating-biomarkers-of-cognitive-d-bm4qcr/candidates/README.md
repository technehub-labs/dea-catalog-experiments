# NCT07226466 — Evaluating Biomarkers of Cognitive Dysfunction in Patients With Cancer

> This study investigates the effects of brain radiotherapy on cognitive function by evaluating plasma biomarkers and apolipoprotein E (APOE) genotype in patients with primary or metastatic brain tumors. Standard brain radiotherapy is known to impact cognitive outcomes, yet the underlying biological mechanisms remain unclear. (NCT NCT07226466; status NOT_YET_RECRUITING).
