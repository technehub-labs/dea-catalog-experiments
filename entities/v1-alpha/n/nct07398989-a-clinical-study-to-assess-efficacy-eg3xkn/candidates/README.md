# NCT07398989 — A Clinical Study to Assess Efficacy and Tolerability of a Topical Skincare Product on Adults With Mature, Crepey Skin

> Subjects having mild-to-moderate crepiness, photodamage, and loss of firmness will apply a topical skincare regimen to the knees, thighs, hands, and all over the body. Evaluations of the regimen's efficacy will be conducted at 4 weeks, 8 weeks, and 12 weeks post-baseline. (NCT NCT07398989; status COMPLETED).
