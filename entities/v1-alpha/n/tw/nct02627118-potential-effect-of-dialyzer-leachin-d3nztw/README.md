# NCT02627118 — Potential Effect of Dialyzer Leaching of BPA From the Fresenius Optiflux 160NR Compared to the Nipro ELISIO-15H

> The aim of the study is to identify hepatic enzyme and estrogen-dependent biochemical changes that occur when dialysis patients are treated with dialyzers known to leach BPA (Bisphenol-A) into the blood, such as the commercially available Fresenius F160NR, as compared with the same chemical evaluations in patients being treated with the non BPA containing Nipro Elisio-15H dialyzer. Evaluations of patient's chemistries will be obtained prior to and after 2 months of standard dialysis treatments with each dialyzer. (NCT NCT02627118; status COMPLETED).
