# NCT06806618 — HAE Burden and Crisis Management

> The study has been designed as a French, multicentric survey study to obtain data to describe the burden of on-demand treatment for patients with hereditary angioedema (HAE). The online survey assesses various aspects of HAE and in particular the burden of HAE from the patients' and caregivers' perspectives. (NCT NCT06806618; status COMPLETED).
