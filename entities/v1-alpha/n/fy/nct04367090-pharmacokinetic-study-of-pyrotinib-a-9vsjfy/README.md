# NCT04367090 — Pharmacokinetic Study of Pyrotinib and Docetaxel in Combination With Trastuzumab in Patients With HER2 Metastatic Breast Cancer

> The primary objective of the study is to study the pharmacokinetics of pyrotinib and docetaxel plus Trastuzumab in HER2 Positive MBC. The secondary objective of the study is to evaluate the safety and efficacy (ORR) of pyrotinib and docetaxel plus Trastuzumab in HER2 Positive MBC. (NCT NCT04367090; status COMPLETED).
