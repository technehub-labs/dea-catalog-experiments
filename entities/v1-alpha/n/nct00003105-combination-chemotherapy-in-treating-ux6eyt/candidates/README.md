# NCT00003105 — Combination Chemotherapy in Treating Patients With Metastatic or Locally Advanced Bladder Cancer

> RATIONALE: Drugs used in chemotherapy use different ways to stop tumor cells from dividing so they stop growing or die. Combining more than one drug may kill more tumor cells. PURPOSE: Phase I/II trial to study the effectiveness of combination chemotherapy in treating patients with metastatic or locally advanced bladder cancer. (NCT NCT00003105; status COMPLETED).
