# NCT05498662 — An Observational Study of the T3 Pro Dental Implant System

> A prospective, observational, multicenter study where 60 patients needing implants in the maxilla or mandible will be enrolled. The patients will be restored with a final prosthesis no later than 4 months following implant placement surgery. The patients will come back for follow-up evaluations yearly for 2 years. (NCT NCT05498662; status ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING).
