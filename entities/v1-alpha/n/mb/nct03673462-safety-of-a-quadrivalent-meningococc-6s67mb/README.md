# NCT03673462 — Safety of a Quadrivalent Meningococcal Conjugate Vaccine Administered Concomitantly With Routine Pediatric Vaccines in Healthy Infants and Toddlers

> The primary objective of this study was to describe the safety profile of Meningococcal Polysaccharide (Serogroups A, C, Y, and W) Tetanus Toxoid (MenACYW) Conjugate Vaccine and Meningococcal (Groups A, C, Y and W 135) Oligosaccharide Diphtheria CRM197 (MENVEO®) Conjugate Vaccine when administered concomitantly with routine pediatric vaccines in healthy infants and toddlers. (NCT NCT03673462; status COMPLETED).
