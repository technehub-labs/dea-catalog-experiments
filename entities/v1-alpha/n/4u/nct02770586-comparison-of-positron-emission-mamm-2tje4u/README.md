# NCT02770586 — Comparison of Positron Emission Mammography and Contrast-enhanced Breast MRI

> The hypothesis of this study is that positron emission mammography will provide comparable sensitivity to contrast-enhanced breast MRI in women with a high suspicion of breast cancer. The aim of this study is to determine the sensitivity of Positron Emission Mammography (PEM) relative to that of Contrast-enhanced breast MRI (CE-MRI) in women with a high suspicion of breast cancer (NCT NCT02770586; status COMPLETED).
