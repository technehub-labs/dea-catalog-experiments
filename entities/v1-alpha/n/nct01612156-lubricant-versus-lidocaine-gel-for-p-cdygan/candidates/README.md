# NCT01612156 — Lubricant Versus Lidocaine Gel for Pain Control During Urodynamics

> The purpose of this study is to determine a difference in patient reported pain levels before, during, and after multichannel urodynamics in patients treated with 2% lidocaine gel or water based lubricant. We hypothesize that lidocaine gel will decrease pain levels when compared to lubricant gel. (NCT NCT01612156; status COMPLETED).
