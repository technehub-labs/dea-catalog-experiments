# NCT03360045 — Comparing Effectiveness of Merocel and Packing With Tranexamic Acid in the Management of Anterior Epistaxis

> Aim of this study is evaluate whether superior nasal compression with tranexamic acid to simple nasal compression and Merocel packing. In this study, patients who presented with non-traumatic anterior epistaxis to emergency departments will be included in this study. Three different therapy option are created; first, nasal compression with tranexamic acid, second, simple nasal compression without any drugs. And third, packing with Merocel. (NCT NCT03360045; status COMPLETED).
