# NCT03428373 — Rivaroxaban or Aspirin As Thromboprophylaxis in Multiple Myeloma

> The intended study is designed as a a phase IV pragmatic multicenter randomized controlled clinical trial, comparing the impact of two different therapies including ASA vs. Rivaroxaban in newly diagnosed or relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma patients treated with Lenalidomide Dexamethasone (Len-Dex) combination therapy. The pilot feasibility study was conducted in preparation for this randomized controlled trial designed to assess the effect of an intervention. (NCT NCT03428373; status UNKNOWN).
