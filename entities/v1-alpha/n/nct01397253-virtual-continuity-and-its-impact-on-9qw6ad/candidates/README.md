# NCT01397253 — Virtual Continuity and Its Impact on Complex Hospitalized Patients' Care

> Communication between physicians caring for a patient in the hospital and that patient's primary care provider is less than optimal, and can lead to diminished health care quality and safety. This project will lead to better communication between physicians and could decrease medication errors that tend to occur as the patient goes from hospital to home. (NCT NCT01397253; status COMPLETED).
