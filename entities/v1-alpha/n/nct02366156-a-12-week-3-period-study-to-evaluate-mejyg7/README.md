# NCT02366156 — A 12 Week, 3-Period Study to Evaluate the Effects of a Dietary Supplement on Lipid Metabolism

> This study will evaluate the effects of encapsulated botanical extracts, previously shown to inhibit the enzyme diacylglycerol-acyltransferase-1 (DGAT-1) in vitro, on fasting and postprandial lipid metabolism during an oral fat tolerance test (OFTT) in apparently healthy, overweight and obese adult men and women.
