# NCT00114686 — Efficacy and Safety of Quetiapine Fumarate (SEROQUEL®) in the Treatment of Alcohol Dependency in Patients With Bipolar Disorder

> The purpose of this study is to determine whether treatment with Quetiapine Fumarate (SEROQUEL) in conjunction with mood stabilizers (Lithium or Divalproex) for 12 weeks helps patients who have Bipolar I Disorder with Alcohol Dependence (NCT NCT00114686; status COMPLETED).
