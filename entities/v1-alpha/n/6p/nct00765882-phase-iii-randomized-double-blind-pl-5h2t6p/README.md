# NCT00765882 — Phase III, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Trial of Linaclotide Administered to Patients With Chronic Constipation

> The objective of this trial is to determine the efficacy and safety of linaclotide administered to patients with chronic constipation (CC). The primary efficacy parameter is the percentage of patients in each treatment group that meet the protocol definition for Complete Spontaneous Bowel Movement (CSBM) Overall Responder (NCT NCT00765882; status COMPLETED).
