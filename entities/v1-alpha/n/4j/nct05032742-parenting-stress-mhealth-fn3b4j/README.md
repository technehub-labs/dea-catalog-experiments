# NCT05032742 — Parenting Stress mHealth

> Parenting stress is a well-documented barrier to youth engagement in community-based substance use treatment. The current project aims to develop and evaluate a mobile health parenting stress intervention for caregivers of justice-involved youth, a population with high rates of substance use and low rates of treatment engagement. (NCT NCT05032742; status COMPLETED).
